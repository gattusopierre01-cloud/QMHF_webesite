Who We Are

Queen Mary Hedge Fund is a student-led investment society at Queen Mary University of London. Founded in 2018, we provide a platform for students to gain practical investment experience and develop their financial knowledge.

Our members come from diverse academic backgrounds, bringing unique perspectives to our investment approach. We believe this diversity is our strength, allowing us to analyze markets from multiple angles and identify opportunities others might miss.

Through rigorous research, educational workshops, and networking events, we aim to prepare our members for successful careers in finance and investment management.

Our Values

Intellectual Curiosity

We foster a culture of continuous learning and intellectual exploration. Our members are encouraged to question assumptions, challenge conventional wisdom, and pursue knowledge with passion.

Analytical Rigor

We believe in thorough analysis and data-driven decision making. Our investment process emphasizes rigorous research, quantitative assessment, and critical thinking.

Collaborative Spirit

We value teamwork and the power of diverse perspectives. By working together and sharing ideas openly, we enhance our collective understanding and achieve better results.

Ethical Conduct

We uphold the highest standards of integrity and ethics in all our activities. We believe that long-term success in finance requires unwavering commitment to ethical principles.

Innovation

We embrace new ideas, technologies, and methodologies. In a rapidly evolving financial landscape, we strive to be at the forefront of innovation in investment analysis and strategy.

Educational Excellence

We are committed to providing exceptional educational opportunities for our members. Through workshops, guest speakers, and hands-on experience, we develop the next generation of financial leaders.
