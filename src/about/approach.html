Our Approach

How we analyze and invest in the markets

Investment Philosophy

At Queen Mary Hedge Fund, we believe in a disciplined, research-driven approach to investing. Our investment philosophy is built on the foundation of thorough fundamental analysis, complemented by macroeconomic insights and quantitative techniques.

We focus on identifying mispriced assets across various markets, seeking to generate consistent returns while managing risk. Our approach combines both top-down and bottom-up methodologies, allowing us to develop a comprehensive view of potential investments.

Rather than following market trends, we conduct independent research to form our own investment theses. This contrarian mindset often leads us to opportunities overlooked by the broader market.

Risk Management

Risk management is at the core of our investment process. We employ a multi-faceted approach to risk, considering not only market volatility but also liquidity, concentration, and correlation risks.

Our portfolio construction process emphasizes diversification across sectors, geographies, and asset classes. We continuously monitor our positions and adjust our allocations based on changing market conditions and risk parameters.

Our Investment Process

1

Idea Generation

We source investment ideas through a combination of screening tools, industry research, and team discussions. Our diverse academic backgrounds allow us to approach problems from multiple perspectives.

2

Fundamental Analysis

Each potential investment undergoes rigorous fundamental analysis, including financial statement review, competitive positioning assessment, and management evaluation.

3

Valuation

We employ multiple valuation methodologies to determine intrinsic value, including discounted cash flow analysis, comparable company analysis, and scenario modeling.

4

Portfolio Construction

Investments are sized based on conviction level, risk parameters, and correlation with existing positions. We maintain a disciplined approach to position sizing and sector allocation.

5

Monitoring & Review

All positions are continuously monitored for changes in fundamentals or valuation. We conduct regular portfolio reviews to ensure alignment with our investment theses.

6

Exit Strategy

Each investment includes a clearly defined exit strategy, with specific price targets and catalysts identified. We maintain discipline in exiting positions when our thesis is realized or invalidated.
